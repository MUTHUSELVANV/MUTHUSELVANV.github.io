A quick glance at the projects behind the roles. From AI tools and hospital simulations at Michigan to production planning at Bharat Electronics, each one taught me something about using tools and applying them efficiently to make the process smoother.
Jan 2025 – Present

Research Associate
Office of the Associate Dean for Undergraduate Education
University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, MI, USA

As a Research Associate in the University of Michigan, I build AI tools, data pipelines, and automated reports so the leadership team doesn't have to pull data by hand. I engineered ETL pipelines connecting Oracle, Google Sheets, and survey datasets into Tableau dashboards that track program outcomes, enrollment patterns, and student engagement across all undergraduate students in the College of Engineering.

I built UM Maizey, a RAG-based system using LangChain and ChromaDB that lets deans ask about workforce outcomes in plain English, and Engineering Explorer, a Claude API chatbot with a LangChain and ChromaDB RAG pipeline that helps prospective students navigate majors and four-year plans, deployed as a web app on U-M ITS Container Service.

I run statistical analysis on enrollment and survey data using SQL and Python, turning the questions deans actually ask into clear answers they can act on. I also work with faculty and academic leadership to understand what they need, then build the visualizations and reports that get them there.

Tools: Python, SQL, R, Tableau, Oracle Database, Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, Qualtrics, SAP Business Objects, Streamlit, LangChain, ChromaDB, Claude API, Git/GitHub

May 2025 – Aug 2025

Systems Engineering Research Intern
Center for Healthcare Engineering & Patient Safety (CHEPS)
University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, MI, USA

Reducing Emergency Department Crowd (REDC)

During my internship, I worked with the Reducing Emergency Department Crowding (REDC) team, a talented group of teammates: Sandra, Grace, Nethra, Laney, and Aimeng, under the guidance of Amy Cohn and Vincenzo Loffredo.

I built a Python-based discrete-event simulation framework to evaluate Emergency Department CT workflows, integrating queueing models, stochastic scenarios, and utilization tracking to replicate real-world operations. I designed and ran over 100 simulation experiments examining the effects of scanner downtime, process delays, and acuity-based patient prioritization strategies on system throughput and efficiency.

The work identified opportunities to free up approximately 24 hours of daily bed capacity, providing hospital leadership with actionable recommendations to make operations more reliable, improve resource allocation, and enhance patient flow.

Tools: Python, Tableau, Microsoft Excel, pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib

Dec 2021 – Feb 2023

Trainee Engineer, Project Management Group
Bharat Electronics Limited
Bangalore, India

I worked in the Project Management Group at Bharat Electronics, a defense electronics manufacturer, supporting the production and delivery of 124,000 VVPATs (Voter Verifiable Paper Audit Trail units) for India's 2024 Parliament elections. My role sat at the intersection of production planning, supply chain coordination, and data analysis, with the goal of keeping a large, time-sensitive manufacturing program on track.

I analyzed production operations data using Python, Excel (VBA and Macros), and Tableau to track material availability, monitor daily throughput against targets, and support execution planning across 8 production lines. Applying Linear Programming and Theory of Constraints to production sequencing, I helped increase daily throughput from 330 to 500 units and eliminated a 140-unit daily WIP pileup, closing an 18% weekly target deficit flagged to leadership.

I built Tableau dashboards integrated with SAP ERP via ETL pipelines to give leadership real-time visibility into inventory levels, assembly progress, quality control checkpoints, and production flow. These dashboards replaced manual status reports and gave the Sales team realistic delivery timelines they could commit to with confidence.

I coordinated with 10 to 15 suppliers on component availability, worked with the SAP PP module to manage MRP runs, EOQ-based lot sizing, and production orders, and implemented a SAP rework tracking code that eliminated duplicate unit counting across stages. I tracked cross-functional delivery milestones across production, quality, and dispatch teams in SAP, keeping every stage aligned to the Election Commission's tight delivery deadline.

Tools: SAP ERP, SAP PP, Python, Excel (VBA, Macros), Tableau
